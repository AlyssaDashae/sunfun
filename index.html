Bahamian Food & Drink

Taste the Islands

Nassau's food culture runs deep — shaped by African, Caribbean, and British roots. These are the dishes and drinks you simply cannot leave without trying.

🍽️ Signature Dishes
🐚 National Dish

Fresh Conch Salad

Raw conch tossed with diced tomatoes, onions, sweet peppers, scotch bonnet, and a bright squeeze of lime and orange juice. Served fresh — practically still from the sea.

Where to try it
Goldie's Conch House ↗ Twin Brothers — Arawak Cay ↗ Arawak Cay Fish Fry ↗
🍜 Island Classic

Conch Chowder

Hearty tomato-based soup (not creamy — think gumbo) packed with conch, potatoes, root vegetables, and island spices. Served piping hot with a side of Johnny Cake to soak it all up.

Where to try it
Cricket Club Restaurant & Pub ↗ The Poop Deck ↗ McKenzie's Fresh Fish & Conch ↗
🔥 Local Favourite

Cracked Conch

Conch meat tenderized with a mallet, dipped in seasoned batter, and deep-fried to golden perfection. Crispy outside, tender inside — served with peas 'n' rice and coleslaw.

Where to try it
Lukka Kairi ↗ The Poop Deck ↗ Twin Brothers ↗
🐟 Sunday Classic

Bahamian Stew Fish

Fresh grouper or snapper marinated in lime, then simmered in a rich tomato-based sauce with onions, thyme, and a medley of island spices. Best eaten with Johnny Cake on the side.

Where to try it
Café Johnny Canoe ↗ Nesbitt's Restaurant & Lounge ↗ Bahama Grill ↗
🦞 Prized Delicacy

Rock Lobster

Spiny lobster pulled straight from the turquoise shallows — smaller and sweeter than its Atlantic cousin. Grilled, steamed, or broiled and served with butter and a squeeze of fresh lime.

Where to try it
The Poop Deck ↗ Solemar ↗ Aquafire ↗
🍚 Island Staple

Peas 'n' Rice

Pigeon peas cooked down with rice, tomatoes, thyme, and sweet peppers — the essential side dish of the Bahamas. You'll find it on virtually every local plate, and for good reason.

Where to try it
Arawak Cay Fish Fry ↗ Nesbitt's Restaurant ↗ Lukka Kairi ↗
🍲 Comfort Food

Sheep Tongue Souse

The Bahamian answer to soup — slow-cooked chicken in a tangy broth of onions, lime, potatoes, and warm spices. A breakfast staple on weekends and a go-to hangover cure for locals.

Where to try it
Nesbitt's Restaurant & Lounge ↗ Café Johnny Canoe ↗ Cricket Club Restaurant & Pub ↗
🍮 Must-Try Dessert

Guava Duff

Soft dough rolled with guava paste, boiled, sliced, and crowned with a warm rum or brandy butter sauce. The iconic Bahamian dessert — sweet, pillowy, and totally unforgettable.

Where to try it
Graycliff Hotel & Restaurant ↗ Café Johnny Canoe ↗ The New Duff ↗
🎂 Take-Home Treat

Bahamian Rum Cake

A dense, moist cake soaked in Ole Nassau rum, available in pineapple, coconut, banana, and chocolate flavors. The Bahamas Rum Cake Factory is the definitive stop for this classic souvenir.

Where to try it
Bahamas Rum Cake Factory ↗ Graycliff Chocolatier ↗
🍹 Island Drinks
🍹
Goombay Smash
The Bahamian Signature

Coconut rum, apricot brandy, pineapple juice, and orange juice — named after the island's own music and dance tradition. No two bars make it exactly the same.

Try it at: Twin Brothers Lukka Kairi
🌅
Bahama Mama
The Classic

Dark rum, coconut liqueur, coffee liqueur, pineapple and orange juice — vibrant, fruity, and completely synonymous with a Bahamian beach day. Often served frozen.

Try it at: Grand Hyatt Baha Mar Blue Sail Bar
🥥
Sky Juice
Born at Arawak Cay

Gin, fresh coconut water, sweetened condensed milk, and nutmeg — the original backyard drink of the Bahamas, invented during Prohibition at Arawak Cay. Creamy, sweet, and deceivingly strong.

Try it at: Arawak Cay Fish Fry Goldie's Conch House
🐦
Yellow Bird
Named for a calypso classic

Light rum, Galliano, crème de banana, pineapple and orange juice — a bright canary-yellow cocktail with a zingy citrus kick. A nod to the beloved Caribbean song of the same name.

Try it at: Frankie Gone Bananas The Poop Deck
🥃
John Watling's Rum
Bahamian Craft Rum

Small-batch rum distilled at Nassau's Buena Vista Estate using hand-harvested Bahamian sugarcane molasses, aged in American white oak. Visit the distillery for a free tour and tasting.

Try it at: John Watling's Distillery
🍺
Kalik Beer
The Local Lager

The Bahamas' own brew — a light, crisp lager that's been a local institution since 1969. Its name comes from the sound of the cowbells in Junkanoo. Cold Kalik, warm sun, white sand: perfection.

Try it at: Pirate Republic Brewing Co. Arawak Cay Fish Fry

Insider Tips

From Our Local Team

01

Best time to snorkel

Head out between 8–11am for calmest waters and best visibility before afternoon breezes pick up.

02

Book excursions early

Pig swimming tours fill up fast — ask the front desk to reserve your spot the evening before.

03

Cash for the market

The Nassau Straw Market is best navigated with small bills in hand. ATMs nearby on Bay Street.

04

Sunset from Cable Beach

A 10-minute walk west brings you to one of the most spectacular sunset views in the Caribbean.
